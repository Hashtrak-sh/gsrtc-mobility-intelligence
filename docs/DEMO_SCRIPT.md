# Demo Script — 15 Minutes

## Before the meeting

- Launch the local app and verify all core pages.
- Generate the latest synthetic data using the documented command.
- Confirm every page shows the simulation badge.
- Keep a PDF report and screenshots available as backup.
- If CCTV inference is unavailable, prepare a transparent explanation rather than pretending the video is live or the counts are model-generated.

## Flow

1. **Context (1 min):** explain the prototype scope and the difference between real map geography and simulated mobility data.
2. **Passenger Flow & O-D (2 min):** show the morning commute pattern and leading origin-destination pairs.
3. **CCTV Analytics (2 min):** show actual processed sample output if available, plus method and limitations. Otherwise explain the optional module's status.
4. **Demand–Supply Gaps (2 min):** select the peak time/location and inspect gap count, capacity, and recommendation.
5. **Demand & Surveys (2 min):** show synthetic reasons for private-mode use and switch conditions.
6. **Route Intelligence (2 min):** inspect a recommended route and expand the score/evidence breakdown.
7. **Competition & Pricing (2 min):** compare illustrative service slots and adjust fare/elasticity to see scenario effects.
8. **Reports (1 min):** export the report and point out assumptions and provenance.
9. **Scale-up (1 min):** explain how approved ETM/ticketing, schedules, GPS, surveys and permitted CCTV inputs would replace synthetic inputs after award.

## Language to use

Say “synthetic demonstration data” and “illustrative scenario” where appropriate. Do not say “live passenger count”, “actual GSRTC ridership”, “AI-validated forecast”, or “real competitor fares” unless those statements are independently true and evidenced.
